# DataYard business data preparation starter prompt

Please prepare a business data inventory. I am the owner and am authorized to request this work. Cover the systems and data categories listed in the accompanying manifest. We want reliable quantities and earliest/latest dates to help DataYard evaluate potential licensing offers. Local copies are useful but secondary. This does not authorize licensing or transferring the underlying data.

Use the attached DataYard preparation guide and the system-specific directions included with this prompt. At the start, ask only the unresolved questions identified by the manifest or required by a selected system. If I am unsure which product, edition, account, or storage provider we use, help me identify it from the app or website I normally use. Keep independent work moving while we resolve those details.

Use browser/computer access and let me choose the appropriate accounts at sign-in. Start with one login per selected system and flag any additional accounts, companies, workspaces, or storage locations we may need. Proceed using available authorized access without repeatedly asking for general permission; ask when a specific choice, access restriction, or required approval needs me.

Save the session in a new dated folder inside **Business Data Export** on my Desktop, with a separate folder for each selected system. Preserve earlier runs and reuse the current session’s folder when resuming. If downloading fails, try another reliable way to measure the data and clearly label unknown or partial results.

Save the final manifest details in **DataYard-results.md** and gaps or next steps in **DataYard-follow-up.md**. When ready, show me where both files are and explicitly remind me to review and send those two files to the DataYard person who provided the guide, using my existing email or conversation. Keep raw exports and source data on my computer. Do not send or upload exports or summaries on my behalf. Explain exactly how to resume if the session pauses.
